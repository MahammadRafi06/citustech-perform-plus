How are these scores calculated?

Quality Impact (0-100) reflects the member's aggregate performance across attributed HEDIS/Stars quality measures, combining current compliance rates, the number and clinical severity of open care gaps, and how actionable those gaps are within the measurement year. Risk Impact (0-100) reflects the member's current RAF, validated and potential HCC recapture/suspect opportunity, and the audit/documentation risk associated with their diagnosis history. Stars Impact (0-100) reflects the weighted contribution of the member's Stars-linked measures, considering measure weight, how close the contract is to the next Star cut point, and the member's individual actionability toward closing those gaps. Opportunity Closure Probability is the model-estimated likelihood that a member's open quality gaps will be closed within the measurement year, based on visit recency, provider closure history, and available clinical evidence; for members with multiple open gaps, this is the average of the individual per-measure probabilities (see each Member 360 for the per-measure breakdown). Composite Impact Score (0-100) = 35% Risk Impact + 25% Quality Impact + 20% Stars Impact + 20% Opportunity Closure Probability, and drives the default sort order in this list.

Why is HCC confidence scored this way?

For Validated, Recapture, Downcoding and Suspect categories, confidence reflects the number and recency of corroborating sources (prior HCC, labs, medications, clinical notes) supporting MEAT (Monitor, Evaluate, Assess, Treat) or the broader TAMPER (Treatment, Assessment, Monitoring, Plan, Evaluate, Referral) documentation framework; items below 85% confidence are excluded from the validated RAF projection until clinician review. For Unsupported HCC / Audit-Risk items, the score instead reflects confidence that the code is currently justified by the record under MEAT/TAMPER - a low score signals elevated audit exposure rather than a new opportunity.

Unsupported HCC / Audit-Risk (Possible Upcoding): an HCC already reflected in Current RAF whose supporting evidence is insufficient (MEAT/TAMPER not demonstrated), creating potential overpayment/clawback exposure rather than upside (per RAF Audit Phase 4, Unsupported HCC Rate testing).

Reconciliation: 0.780 + 0.000 validated = 0.780 projected. 0.780 + 0.120 total raw opportunity (excludes audit-risk) = 0.900 potential.

Illustrative value uses a demonstration rate of $12,000 per RAF point and is not an actual plan-specific PMPM or revenue figure.

Why is HCC confidence scored this way?

For Validated, Recapture, Downcoding and Suspect categories, confidence reflects the number and recency of corroborating sources (prior HCC, labs, medications, clinical notes) supporting MEAT (Monitor, Evaluate, Assess, Treat) or the broader TAMPER (Treatment, Assessment, Monitoring, Plan, Evaluate, Referral) documentation framework; items below 85% confidence are excluded from the validated RAF projection until clinician review. For Unsupported HCC / Audit-Risk items, the score instead reflects confidence that the code is currently justified by the record under MEAT/TAMPER - a low score signals elevated audit exposure rather than a new opportunity.
